## Get in touch

I welcome inquiries across the following areas:

- **Research collaboration** — If you are working on XR, QoE, psychophysiology, or digital health and see overlap with the lab's work, I am happy to discuss joint projects, data sharing, or grant applications. Please include a brief description of your research and what collaboration you have in mind.
- **Thesis supervision (Master's / Bachelor's)** — HSHL students interested in VR/AR user studies, physiological measurement, or digital health applications are encouraged to reach out. Include your area of interest and current semester.
- **Industry partnerships & transfer** — The lab has experience deploying XR systems in clinical, industrial, and public-space settings. If you are exploring applied R&D or pilot studies, please describe your use case.
- **Press, media & invited talks** — For interview requests, expert commentary on XR/AI topics, or speaking invitations, please contact via email with event details and timeline.
