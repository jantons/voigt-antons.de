## Why standardization

Standardization defines how a field measures itself: without agreed test methods, results from different labs cannot be compared. Contributing to standardization means shaping those shared methods before they are decided elsewhere. This is a small and specialized part of academic output — most researchers publish papers; few also sit on the standards bodies that define how their field measures quality.

## ITU-T Recommendation P.812

Co-developer of **ITU-T P.812**, *Principles of subjective test methods for interactive virtual reality (VR) applications* (2024) — an ITU-T recommendation defining how subjective quality tests for interactive VR should be designed and conducted.
