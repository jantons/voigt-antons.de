This contribution outlines DemTab, a tablet-based solution to assist guideline-oriented treatment planning, self-management, and communication in outpatient dementia care. We describe core modules, stakeholder involvement, and early feasibility feedback from primary care practices. Initial experiences suggest improved transparency and coordination, alongside requirements for training and data security.
