Professor of Computer Science · Immersive Media
Prof. Dr.-Ing. Jan-Niklas Voigt-Antons

Making immersive systems measurably better.

I study how virtual and augmented reality systems can be built so they actually work for people. My work pairs EEG, eye tracking and physiological sensing with XR deployments in hospitals, public space and safety-critical training — measuring what questionnaires cannot capture.

Professor at Hamm-Lippstadt University of Applied Sciences, Director of the Immersive Reality Lab, and Guest Researcher at TU Berlin.

01

Research lines

Most XR research either studies interaction in the lab or measures physiology separately. I do both together — and take XR out of the lab, into hospitals, public spaces and safety-critical training environments.

XR & Spatial Interaction

Spatial interaction that holds up outside the lab — usable, socially acceptable, deployment-ready.

embodied interactionsocial acceptabilitymixed reality

Quality of Experience

Linking technical system behaviour to perceived quality, trust and adoption — streaming to VR cloud gaming.

network impairmentsquestionnairesstatistical modelling

Psychophysiology & Behaviour

EEG, eye tracking and behavioural coding as a complement to self-report for robust user-state assessment.

eye trackingmultimodal sensingworkload

Digital Health & Learning

Human-centered XR and serious games that measurably improve engagement and training outcomes.

serious gameshealth communicationtraining

Generative AI in Media

Embodied agents and synthetic media enter health and training faster than methods to evaluate trust in them.
