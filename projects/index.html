2008–2014

Bernstein Focus Neurotechnology — Berlin

neurotechnologybrain readingbiosignalsusability

Role: Project Lead · Funding: Bernstein Focus, Bundesministerium für Bildung und Forschung · Period: 07/2008 – 02/2014

Neurotechnologies for improving intuitive and efficient human–machine interaction. The Bernstein Focus: Neurotechnology Berlin brought together a multidisciplinary faculty to apply insights from basic neuroscience directly to real-world applications, with a focus on non-invasive „brain reading" techniques.

Within it, project A3 „Neuro-Usability": Neurotechnologies for Human-Machine Interaction used biosignal sensors to measure and model aspects of usability — efficiency, satisfaction and joy of use. This is where my psychophysiological measurement approach originates.

Representative outputs

  • Single-trial analysis of the neural correlates of speech quality perception — Journal of Neural Engineering
  • Analyzing Speech Quality Perception using Electro-Encephalography — IEEE JSTSP
