DIDYMOS-XR

digital twinsXRAIethics-by-design

Role: Project Lead · Funding: HORIZON Action Grant, European Union

Dynamic and responsible digital twins for extended reality: building twins that stay synchronised with the physical world and are evaluated for user experience, not only for geometric fidelity. Two innovations from this project were recognised as Key Innovator entries on the European Commission's Innovation Radar in 2024.

MIA-PROM

digital healthPROMvirtual assistantaccessibility

Role: Project Lead · Funding: KIAS, Bundesministerium für Bildung und Forschung

Multimodal, interactive assistance for the digital collection of patient-reported outcome measures — comparing embodied avatars, robots and voice assistants in rehabilitation settings, with cross-randomised controlled trials at Charité Berlin and partner clinics.
