Too Tired for Calling? A Physiological Measure of Fatigue Caused by Bandwidth Limitations

Antons, J.-N., Schleicher, R., Arndt, S., Möller, S. & Curio, G.

Poster presented at the 4th Int. Workshop on Quality of Multimedia Experience (QoMEX 2012), Yarra Valley, Australia

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

We evaluate how bandwidth-induced quality reductions in speech communication affect user fatigue. Physiological measures including heart rate variability and blink activity are collected alongside subjective reports. Findings indicate that constrained bandwidth conditions lead to higher fatigue compared to full-bandwidth communication, emphasizing the importance of considering long-term effects of quality impairments.
