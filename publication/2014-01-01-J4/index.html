Methods for Assessing the Quality of Transmitted Speech and of Speech Communication Services

Köster, F., Möller, S., Antons, J.-N., Arndt, S., Guse, D. & Weiss, B.

Acoustics Australia, 42, 179–184

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

This article reviews different methods for evaluating transmitted speech and speech communication services. It compares subjective listening tests, instrumental measures, and hybrid approaches, emphasizing the importance of context-specific evaluation for improving communication quality.
