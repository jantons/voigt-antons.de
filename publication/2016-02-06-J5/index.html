Using Electroencephalography to Analyze Sleepiness due to Low-quality Audiovisual Stimuli

Arndt, S., Antons, J.-N., Schleicher, R., & Möller, S.

Signal Processing: Image Communication, 42, 120-129

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

The paper examines how exposure to low-quality audiovisual stimuli affects sleepiness, using EEG measurements to identify neural correlates. Results indicate that poor media quality increases drowsiness, suggesting important implications for user safety and media consumption contexts.
