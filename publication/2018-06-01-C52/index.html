„OurPuppet“ – Entwicklung einer Mensch-Technik-Interaktion für die Unterstützung informell Pflegender

Dimitrov, T., Kramps, O., Naroska, E., Bolten, T., Demmer, J., Ressel, C., Könen, S., Matthies, O., Habibi, A., Polzehl, T., Voigt-Antons, J.-N., Matip, E.-M., Mertens, J. & Heutelbeck, D.

Paper presented at the 1st cluster conference „Future of Nursing Care“, Oldenburg, Germany

BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

This contribution introduces “OurPuppet,” a human–technology interaction concept designed to support informal caregivers in routine tasks and emotional engagement. The system combines tangible interaction with assistive prompts and remote supervision features to reduce cognitive load and promote adherence to care routines. Early formative evaluations with caregivers indicated high perceived usefulness and approachable interaction, informing next design iterations.
