Tablet-Based Outpatient Care for People With Dementia - The DemTab Study Protocol

Lech, S., O’Sullivan, J. L., Gellert, P., Voigt-Antons, J.-N. & Nordheim J.

GeroPsych, 32(3), 135-144

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

This article outlines the study protocol for the DemTab project, which investigates tablet-based outpatient care for people with dementia. The project aims to improve guideline-based treatment planning, individualized disease management, and communication among care providers using digital tools.
