QUALINET White Paper on Definitions of Immersive Media Experience (IMEx). arXiv preprint arXiv:2007.07032

Perkis, A., Timmerer, C., Baraković, S., Husić, J. B., Bech, S., Bosse, S., ... , Voigt-Antons, J.-N., …

arXiv preprint arXiv:2007.07032

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

The white paper proposes shared terminology and conceptual dimensions for Immersive Media Experience (IMEx), spanning technological factors, user states, and context. It consolidates community perspectives and offers recommendations for interoperable evaluation methods to advance research and standardization in immersive media.
