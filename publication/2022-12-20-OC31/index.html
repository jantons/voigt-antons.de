Dementia and the role of evidence-based guidelines in primary care – Results of a cluster randomised controlled trial

Lech, S., O´Sullivan, J. L., Spang, R. P., Voigt-Antons, J.-N., Gellert, P. & Nordheim, J.

Paper presented at the GSA Annual Scientific Meeting

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

This presentation reports primary care outcomes from a cluster-randomized trial evaluating adherence to dementia care guidelines. We analyze documentation quality, referral patterns, and patient-reported outcomes under guideline-oriented support versus usual care. Findings suggest that structured guideline implementation improves key process measures and may facilitate earlier interventions along the care pathway.
