Effectiveness of a tablet-based intervention for people living with dementia in primary care – A Cluster Randomized Controlled Trial

Lech, S., Gellert, P., Spang, R., Voigt-Antons, J.-N., Huscher, D., O'Sullivan, J. L., Schuster, J.

International Journal of Geriatric Psychiatry. 38, 12, e6035

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

This cluster randomized controlled trial evaluated the effectiveness of a tablet-based intervention for people with dementia in primary care. The study found improvements in guideline adherence, patient activation, and care processes, supporting the potential of digital tools in dementia care.
