Investigating the impact of virtual element misalignment in collaborative Augmented Reality experiences

Vona, F., Hinzmann, S. L., Stern, M., Ćeranić, M., Grieshammer, D. K. & Voigt-Antons, J.-N.

Paper presented at the 16th International Conference on Quality of Multimedia Experience (QoMEX 2024), Karlshamn, Sweden

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

We study how spatial misalignment and temporal delay between shared virtual objects affect collaborative presence and team performance in AR. Increasing offsets degraded coordination quality, trust, and usability ratings. We discuss tolerance bounds for alignment and latency and propose calibration and synchronization strategies for co-located AR.
