The influence of the level of detail and interactivity of 3D elements on UX in XR applications

Vergari, M., Kojić, T., Warsinke, M., Möller, S., Voigt-Antons, J.-N., Xiao, X. & Abboud, O.

Paper presented at the International Conference on Human-Computer Interaction (HCII 2024). Washington DC, USA

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

This study disentangles the effects of geometric detail and interaction richness on user experience in XR. Through factorial experiments, we measure presence, workload, performance, and cybersickness across combinations of mesh complexity and interaction depth. Findings guide resource allocation decisions: when to invest in fidelity versus interactive affordances for maximal UX gain.
