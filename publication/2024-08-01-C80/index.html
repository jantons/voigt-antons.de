Enhancing Job Interview Preparation Through Immersive Experiences Using Photorealistic, AI-powered Metahuman Avatars

Ashrafi, N., Vona, F., Ringsdorf, C., Vergari, M., Schorlemmer, J., Marquardt, M., & Voigt-Antons, J.-N.

Paper presented at the 23rd IEEE International Symposium on Mixed and Augmented Reality (ISMAR 2024), Greater Seattle Area, USA

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

We present an immersive interview-training system that uses photorealistic, AI-driven metahuman avatars to simulate challenging interviewer behaviors. In a formative study, participants reported higher engagement and self-efficacy compared to non-immersive practice, while noting potential uncanny-valley effects. We outline design trade-offs between realism, responsiveness, and user comfort for coaching applications.
