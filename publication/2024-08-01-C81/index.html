VR Cloud Gaming UX: Exploring the Impact of Network Quality on Emotion, Presence, Game Experience and Cybersickness

Warsinke, M., Kojic, T., Vergari, M. & Voigt-Antons, J.-N. & Möller, S.

Paper presented at the 23rd IEEE International Symposium on Mixed and Augmented Reality (ISMAR 2024), Greater Seattle Area, USA

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

We study how controlled degradations of latency, bitrate, and packet loss in cloud-rendered VR affect user experience. Behavioral metrics and self-reports show that network impairments reduce presence and enjoyment while increasing cybersickness and negative affect. We discuss threshold regions and provide recommendations for adaptive streaming strategies to preserve VR gaming quality under fluctuating networks.
