Size Matters: The Impact of Avatar Size on User Experience in Healthcare Applications

Ashrafi, N., Vona, F., Hinzmann, S. L., Henning, J., Vergari, M., Warsinke, M., Moreira, C. P. & Voigt-Antons, J.-N.

Paper presented at the 17th International Conference on Quality of Multimedia Experience (QoMEX 2025), Madrid, Spain

DOI / paper ↗BibTeX ↓All publications

Abstract

This paper investigates how avatar size influences user experience in healthcare-related virtual environments. By testing different avatar scales, the study explores effects on presence, empathy, and task performance, offering design guidelines for XR healthcare applications.
